# Fancy Privacy Notice Draft

Status: DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW — NOT EFFECTIVE. Prepared 21 October 2026.

This proposed notice covers Fancy's first release for adults meeting over meals in London. It describes the intended service, not a claim that external integrations or deletion processes have passed release checks. Publication depends on the decisions in [the review checklist](README.md).

## Information we collect

We receive information you give us when registering, completing your profile, arranging or applying to join meals, messaging, reporting attendance, leaving feedback, completing an optional product survey or contacting support. This includes login identifiers, email, public profile fields, meal arrangements, applications, messages, feedback and support records.

Our login providers supply authentication identifiers and permitted account details, such as a verified email address. Other members may provide attendance reports, reviews or reports involving you. We collect security and service information such as IP address, timestamps, session identifiers, errors and, where enabled with the appropriate choice, usage events. Restaurant information comes from our places provider and member arrangements.

Account and required profile information are needed to participate. Browsing public content does not require an account. Optional profile details, survey responses and AI use are voluntary. Declining optional information may limit the corresponding personalisation, without preventing core participation.

## Why we use it

The following is the proposed purpose and lawful-basis mapping, subject to approval before release:

| Purpose | Proposed basis |
| --- | --- |
| Authenticate you and provide requested meal, application and messaging features | Performance of our contract with you |
| Send necessary login and meal-status messages | Performance of our contract with you |
| Prevent abuse, investigate reports and protect service security | Legitimate interests in protecting members and operating a trustworthy service, following an assessment |
| Process optional feedback and evaluate service quality | Legitimate interests in improving the service, following an assessment |
| Optional analytics or storage that requires consent | Consent, with an equally accessible withdrawal choice |
| Meet an applicable legal requirement | Legal obligation, identified for the particular processing |

Agreement to this notice is not blanket consent. Where consent is required, we ask separately. Dietary information may reveal health or religious information. Before collecting or using such information, we must identify an additional special-category condition and implement the necessary choice; entering a dietary preference alone is not treated as explicit consent.

## What other members can see

Visitors can see the public profile fields you choose to provide, public meal information and published personal reviews. Do not include private contact details in public fields.

Application notes are shared with the relevant host. Group messages are available to members within their authorised membership periods; direct messages are available to authorised participants. The operator may access relevant records to resolve a support or safety case. Messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

Private whole-meal scores and product-survey answers are restricted to you and authorised people performing analysis or support. A one-sided choice to meet someone again is not disclosed to that person. Mutual choices can enable a connection under the service's eligibility rules. Attendance reports and corrections affect feedback and connection eligibility; contact support if a record is wrong.

## AI assistance

If enabled, the assistant can use your request, relevant meal facts and permitted saved preferences to suggest meals or prepare text. It does not autonomously send messages, approve applications or publish meals. You review and submit the final action.

The proposed model provider is DeepSeek. Real user input must not be sent until the provider's processing terms, locations, retention, training use and any required transfer safeguards are verified and described here. Send only information needed for the requested assistance. Do not include another person's private information or sensitive details in prompts.

The application's proposed AI session design clears temporary server-side conversation content after 30 minutes of inactivity or a new conversation, and a restart may end the session. This is not a promise that the model provider erases its copies within 30 minutes. Saved account preferences are separate and can be managed or deleted. Sending content to a provider cannot subsequently be undone by clearing the local conversation.

## Storage and deletion

We retain account and participation information for the service relationship and the limited periods needed for support, security, disputes and applicable legal duties. Exact category-specific periods, backup expiry and any exceptions must be approved and added before publication; old draft periods are not approved by this notice.

Deleting your account starts a verified deletion process, ends sessions and removes new participation and contact permissions. Upcoming arrangements may be cancelled or exited. Shared meal history and others' records may remain after your identity and personal content are removed or anonymised. Only justified minimum records may be retained for an approved purpose. Provider failures can delay completion and must be reflected in the deletion status. Backup recovery must reapply completed deletion instructions.

## Cookies and your choices

Essential session and security storage supports login and safe requests. Before release, our storage inventory must identify any optional analytics or similar technology, its purpose and duration, and the relevant consent controls. Necessary operational emails are separate from marketing; using Fancy does not subscribe you to marketing.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your information. Contact [PUBLIC SUPPORT EMAIL]; there need not be an in-app export button for you to make a request. We may ask for proportionate identity verification.

**You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.** Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. We explain any applicable limits or justified refusal and respond within the applicable legal timeframe.

You can complain to the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) without first contacting us. Fancy is intended for people aged 18 or over; contact us if you believe an underage person is using it. The assistant provides suggestions, not solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Any material change to this notice will be dated and brought to your attention where required.
