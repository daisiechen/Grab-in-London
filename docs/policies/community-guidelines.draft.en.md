# Fancy Community Guidelines Draft

Status: DRAFT FOR OWNER REVIEW — NOT EFFECTIVE. Prepared 21 October 2026; report handling updated 29 October 2026 to match the implemented moderation tools.

Fancy helps adults meet new people over a meal. These proposed rules apply to profiles, meal listings, applications, chats, reviews and interactions connected with a Fancy meal. They do not replace a separate service agreement or privacy notice.

## Be honest and respectful

You must be at least 18. Use accurate information about yourself and your plans. Do not impersonate someone, create deceptive accounts or make misleading claims about attendance, bookings or other members. Fancy is for social connection; participation is not consent to romantic, sexual or commercial approaches.

No harassment, threats, hate, discrimination, sexual coercion, stalking or repeated unwanted contact. Respect a refusal or a decision to stop contact. Do not use the service for scams, unsolicited advertising, illegal activity or exploitation.

## Make meal plans clear

Hosts should state the restaurant, time, expected budget and arrangements accurately, and keep participants informed of material changes. A listing is not proof of a restaurant reservation. Verify the reservation and any changes directly where needed.

Apply only when you intend to attend. If plans change, withdraw or leave promptly so others can plan. Hosts should make participation decisions respectfully and must not demand sensitive personal information. Do not pressure anyone to lend money or transfer funds. The first release does not collect restaurant payments or book tables for members; agree costs clearly and pay the restaurant under its own arrangements.

## Protect privacy

Do not post another person's contact details, location, private messages, photographs or sensitive information without permission. Do not publish private attendance or support records. Ask before photographing or sharing images of other people. Never share login codes or account credentials.

## Meet with care

Meet at the stated public venue, keep control of your transport and belongings, and leave if you feel uncomfortable. Profiles and attendance records do not constitute background checks or guarantees about another person.

Discuss dietary needs directly with the restaurant. A member's description or restaurant label cannot guarantee allergen safety. Do not pressure anyone to disclose a medical condition or religious belief.

## Give fair feedback

Review only an experience you are eligible to review, describe what happened honestly and avoid insults or unsupported accusations. Do not threaten poor reviews, trade favourable reviews, retaliate or try to manipulate attendance and feedback records. Private scores and meet-again choices are separate from public reviews. Ask support to review an inaccurate attendance record rather than inventing a new report.

## Report problems and respect boundaries

Use the in-app report and block controls where available, or contact [PUBLIC SUPPORT EMAIL]. Include the relevant meal, profile or message and a brief factual description. A receipt acknowledges your report; it is not confirmation that an investigation is complete.

Blocking does not automatically remove either person from a shared group. Use the available leave/report options and contact support where needed. Fancy support is not an emergency service or continuously monitored crisis channel. For immediate danger in the UK, call 999 or 112.

## How reports are handled

When you report a profile, meal or message, the reported item is hidden from you straight away, and you get a receipt in the app. If several different members report the same item within seven days, it is limited for everyone (a message's text is hidden, a meal is removed from discovery and stops accepting applications, a profile's bio and photo are hidden) while the operator reviews it. These limits can be reversed.

The operator reviews reports and relevant evidence proportionately. Possible outcomes are keeping or lifting a limit, removing content such as a profile name, bio or photo, cancelling a meal listing, or suspending an account. A suspended account cannot sign in or use the service and its content is hidden from other members; a suspension can be lifted after review. Every decision is recorded internally.

Where appropriate, we explain the outcome in the report status without exposing the reporter or private information. A report that receives no further review within 30 days is closed; any limit already applied stays in place. You can request reconsideration through [PUBLIC SUPPORT EMAIL]. A different outcome is not guaranteed; new information will be considered. No report guarantees a particular response time or outcome. We may preserve only information justified by the approved privacy and retention policy.
