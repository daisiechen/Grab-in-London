Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Fancy is for adults who want to meet new people over a good meal. These guidelines apply to profiles, photos, meal listings, applications, chats, reviews and anything connected with a Fancy meal, including the meal itself. They are part of our [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.draft.en.md). Fancy has **zero tolerance** for abuse: breaking these guidelines can mean losing your account.

## Be yourself

- You must be 18 or over.
- Use your real first name or the name people know you by, and a recent photo of yourself, or no photo. No group photos, photos of other people, children, celebrities or logos.
- Keep your profile honest. Don't impersonate anyone or create more than one account.
- Keep private details out of public places: no phone numbers, addresses, social media handles or email addresses in your profile, meal listings or reviews.

## Respect everyone at the table

- Fancy is about friendship. Taking part is not an invitation to romantic, sexual or business approaches.
- No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination, stalking or repeated unwanted contact, on or off the app.
- No sexual content, nudity or sexual comments.
- No violent or graphic content, and nothing that encourages self-harm.
- If someone says no, leaves a chat or stops replying, respect it.
- No scams, spam, selling, advertising or asking people for money.

## Hosting a meal

- Describe the restaurant, time, expected budget per person and plans accurately.
- Book the table yourself, directly with the restaurant, and only say it is booked when it is. Update your booking statement if the number of people changes.
- Choose guests fairly and respectfully. Don't ask anyone for sensitive information such as health, religion or sexuality.
- If plans change, update or cancel the meal in the app as early as you can and let your group know.

## Joining a meal

- Apply only when you mean to come. Your note to the host should be friendly and relevant.
- If you can't make it, leave the meal in the app as early as possible so someone else can take your seat.
- Fancy doesn't take payments. Everyone pays the restaurant for their own order unless your group agrees otherwise. Never pressure anyone to pay for you or lend you money.

## Stay safe

- Meet at the restaurant listed for the meal. Plan your own way there and home, and keep your belongings with you.
- Profiles, reviews and attendance records are not background checks. Trust your instincts and leave if you feel uncomfortable.
- Talk to the restaurant directly about allergies and dietary needs. A meal's dietary label is not an allergen guarantee.
- Don't share sign-in codes or let anyone else use your account.
- If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 (or 112).

## Protect other people's privacy

Don't share other members' messages, photos, contact details or personal information, inside or outside Fancy, without their permission. Ask before taking photos of anyone at the table, and before posting them anywhere.

## Leave honest feedback

- Review only people you actually ate with, describe what happened fairly and keep it respectful.
- Don't trade, threaten or retaliate with reviews, and don't misreport attendance.
- If an attendance record about you is wrong, contact us rather than posting about it.

## Reporting and blocking

- **Report** a member or a meal with “Report this profile” or “Report this meal” in the app. To report something said in a chat, report the member who said it and tell us what was said. Tell us what happened; your report is private and the person you report is not told who reported them. You receive a reference so you can check its status.
- **Block** any member to stop private contact with them. Blocking does not remove either of you from a group chat you share, so leave the chat or report the member if you need to.
- You can also email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## What happens when something is reported

- We do not check content before it is posted, so reports matter. When you report a profile or a meal, it is hidden from you straight away and you get a receipt in the app.
- If several different members report the same item within seven days, it is limited for everyone while we review it: a meal is removed from discovery and stops accepting applications, and a profile's bio and photo are hidden. These limits can be reversed.
- We aim to review reports of objectionable content within 24 hours. We look at the content and any relevant records.
- Depending on what happened, we may keep or lift a limit, remove content such as a profile name, bio or photo, cancel a meal listing, or suspend an account. A suspended account cannot sign in and its content is hidden from other members. Serious breaches, such as threats, sexual harassment, hate or involving anyone under 18, lead to immediate removal from Fancy. Where there is a risk to someone's safety, we may contact the police.
- Automatic limits can be reversed, and decisions to suspend an account are made by a person. Every decision is recorded internally. A report that receives no further review within 30 days is closed; any limit already applied stays in place.
- If you disagree with a decision about your content or account, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 30 days. We will look at it again and tell you the outcome.

Fancy support is not an emergency service. For emergencies, call 999.
