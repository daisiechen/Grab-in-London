## 1. What Fancy is

Fancy helps adults meet new people over a meal in London. Members can host a meal at a restaurant of their choice, apply to join other members' meals, chat with the people at their table and, after the meal, choose whether to stay in touch.

Fancy is a place to arrange meals. It is not a restaurant, a booking agent, a dating service, an events organiser or an emergency service. We are not a party to any meal or to any arrangement between members and restaurants.

## 3. Meals, bookings and money

- **Hosts book the table.** A host chooses the restaurant and is responsible for making, changing and cancelling any reservation directly with the restaurant, outside Fancy. A host's statement that a table is booked is the host's own statement; we do not check it.
- **Everyone pays the restaurant.** Fancy does not take payments, deposits or fees, and does not handle refunds. Each person pays the restaurant directly for what they order, on the restaurant's terms. The budget shown on a meal is the host's estimate per person, not a price.
- **No fees for cancelling.** Hosts and guests can cancel or leave through the app. Please do so as early as possible and follow the Community Guidelines. A meal is cancelled automatically if not enough people have confirmed seats by the application deadline.
- **Restaurant information.** Restaurant names, addresses, photos and maps come from Google Maps Platform and from hosts. They may be out of date or wrong. Check details with the restaurant.
- **Food and allergies.** Dietary labels on a meal describe the host's plans, not a guarantee. Talk to the restaurant directly about allergies and dietary needs.
- **Meeting people.** We do not run background or identity checks on members. Profiles, reviews and attendance records are not guarantees about anyone. Use your own judgement and follow the safety tips in the Community Guidelines.
