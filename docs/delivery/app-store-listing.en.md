## Description (4,000)

Fancy a bite?

Fancy helps adults meet new people over a meal in London. Find a small table at a restaurant near you, say hello to the host, and share dinner with a small group of up to six people you haven't met yet. Or host your own table at a place you love.

FIND A TABLE
• Browse meals across central London on a map or as a list.
• Filter by area, date, language, group size, budget, cuisine and dietary options.
• See the restaurant, time, expected budget per person and who's coming before you apply.

HOST YOUR OWN
• Pick any restaurant, choose a time and set a small group size.
• Write a few lines about the evening and the kind of company you hope for.
• Review applications and choose your table. You book the restaurant yourself, and everyone pays for their own meal at the venue.

PLAN TOGETHER
• Once you have a seat, join your table's group chat to sort out the details.
• Leave or cancel in a tap if your plans change, so someone else can take your seat.

STAY IN TOUCH, IF YOU BOTH WANT TO
• After the meal, say who was there and leave a short, honest review.
• Choose who you'd like to meet again. A private conversation opens only when you both choose each other.

SAFE AND RESPECTFUL
• Fancy is for adults 18 and over, and for friendship.
• Report any profile or meal, and block anyone, straight from the app.
• Zero tolerance for harassment, hate and unwanted advances.
• Your email address is never shown to other members.

NO FEES
Fancy doesn't take payments or deposits. Hosts book the table directly with the restaurant, and everyone pays the restaurant for what they order.

Fancy is a new, independent service made in London. We'd love to hear what you think: support and policies are at fancyabite.uk/support.

Fancy helps adults (18+) arrange small group meals at London restaurants. There are no payments in the app: hosts book the restaurant themselves and everyone pays the venue directly.
