This notice explains how Fancy collects and uses personal information when you use the Fancy iOS app, the Fancy website at fancyabite.uk and related emails. It is written for the first release of Fancy, a service that helps adults meet new people over a meal in London.

Who we are

Fancy is run by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, an individual based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described here, which means we decide why and how it is used.

  • Email: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}
  • Postal contact address: {{OPERATOR_CONTACT_ADDRESS}}
  • ICO registration: {{ICO_REGISTRATION}}

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because the law does not require one for a service of this size. Questions about this notice or your information go to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • You can browse meals and public profiles without an account. To host, apply, chat or leave feedback you need an account and a profile.
  • Your name, profile photo, introduction, interests, languages and any optional background you add are public. Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • We do not sell your information, show advertising or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Product analytics are off unless you allow them, and you can change your choice in Settings at any time.
  • Fancy does not take payments. Hosts book restaurants outside Fancy and everyone pays the restaurant directly.
  • Our servers and database are in Seoul, Republic of Korea. The UK recognises Korea as providing adequate protection for personal data.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account).

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in and security data: account and session identifiers, sign-in method, sign-in times and expiry, and hashed (not readable) versions of your IP address and email address used to limit repeated sign-in attempts and abuse.
  • Service records: timestamps of actions such as applying, approving or leaving a meal, which members held a seat, notifications we created for you and whether you have read them.
  • Server logs: our hosting and security providers process your IP address and basic request details (such as time, address requested and errors) to deliver and protect the service.
  • Product analytics, only with your permission: after you sign in, the app asks whether you allow analytics. If you say yes, we record a limited set of events on our own server, for example which filters you used when browsing meals (such as area, dates, budget, group size, language, mood, cuisine or dietary option) and which meals were shown, that a meal card was seen, a meal page or profile was opened, the map was opened, or a step of the create-meal form had an error. Events are linked to your account and a random identifier that changes with each app session. They never contain message text, form text or your location. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising software. You can turn analytics off in Settings; we then stop collecting new events.

The app does not collect your device's location, contacts, advertising identifier, health data or payment details. It asks for access to your photo library only when you choose to add a profile photo.

Information from others

  • Google and Apple, if you sign in with them: a unique identifier for your account with them and your email address (and whether it is verified). We do not receive your password or access to your Google or Apple data.
  • Other members: hosts' decisions on your application, attendance reports, reviews about you, and reports that involve you.
  • Google Maps Platform: restaurant names, addresses, locations and photos used when a host chooses a restaurant and when meals are displayed.

International transfers

Our servers and database are located in the Republic of Korea. The UK government has made adequacy regulations for the Republic of Korea (the Data Protection (Adequacy) (Republic of Korea) Regulations 2022, the "UK–Korea data bridge"), which means personal information can be transferred there from the UK without additional safeguards.

Some providers listed above are based in, or may access information from, other countries, including the United States. Where a transfer goes to a country without UK adequacy regulations, we rely on safeguards recognised by UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. We ask you to confirm your identity first. When deletion starts, you are signed out everywhere, upcoming meals you host are cancelled (their members are told), you give up your seats at upcoming meals, pending applications are withdrawn, you leave your conversations, and people can no longer contact you through Fancy. You can check the progress of your request in Settings → Check deletion status, and we email you when it is received and when it is completed.

When we delete your account we:

  • delete your email address, profile, profile photo, product survey answers, private meal scores, analytics events, sessions and linked sign-in identities, and ask Auth0 to delete your sign-in account;
  • delete reviews you wrote and reviews written about you;
  • replace the text of your messages with "Message removed" and your application notes with a removal notice, and replace the title and description of meals you hosted;
  • keep a minimal record that an account took part in past meals (for example, that a seat was held and attendance was recorded), with no name, photo, email or written content, so that other members' histories stay accurate.

Safety records are the exception. If your account is involved in a report, whether the report is about you or you made it, we keep that report and the records needed to handle it: what the reporter told us, a copy of the reported content (profile text, meal details, or your messages close to a reported message), and our notes and decisions. We keep them for 12 months after the case is closed, or 12 months after your account is deleted if that is later, and then delete them. We do not keep your profile photo. These records are no longer linked to your email address or sign-in, only staff handling the case can see them, and we use them only to protect members, handle disputes and complaints, and deal with repeated abuse (legitimate interests).

We may also keep the minimum information needed for a legal claim or a legal obligation, for as long as that need lasts. We may keep your email address briefly to send the confirmation emails about your deletion.

Cookies and storage on your device

The app stores your sign-in session securely in the iOS Keychain and keeps a small file so it can tell when it has been reinstalled. The Fancy website uses a strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in and protect against cross-site request forgery. These are needed for the service to work and do not require consent. The legal pages on fancyabite.uk set no cookies. Analytics, if you allow them, are controlled by the in-app choice described above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information (you can edit most of your profile in the app);
  • delete your information (you can delete your account in the app);
  • restrict how we use your information in some circumstances;
  • data portability, to receive information you gave us in a reusable format;
  • object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning analytics off in Settings. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew.

To make a request, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. There is no export button in the app, but you can still ask for a copy of your information. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the email address on your account. We respond within one month, or tell you if we need longer as the law allows. There is normally no fee.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

Age limit

Fancy is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using Fancy, please tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or use the report button on their profile, and we will close the account.
