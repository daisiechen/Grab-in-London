1. What Fancy is

Fancy helps adults meet new people over a meal in London. Members can host a meal at a restaurant of their choice, apply to join other members' meals, chat with the people at their table and, after the meal, choose whether to stay in touch.

Fancy is a place to arrange meals. It is not a restaurant, a booking agent, a dating service, an events organiser or an emergency service. We are not a party to any meal or to any arrangement between members and restaurants.

  • Everyone pays the restaurant. Fancy does not take payments, deposits or fees, and does not handle refunds. Each person pays the restaurant directly for what they order, on the restaurant's terms. The budget shown on a meal is the host's estimate per person, not a price.
