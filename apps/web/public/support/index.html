Need help with Fancy? Here is how to reach us and how to sort out the most common things yourself.

Contact us

  • Email: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}
  • Operator: {{OPERATOR_NAME}}, {{OPERATOR_CONTACT_ADDRESS}}

Fancy is run by one person. We read every message and aim to reply within {{SUPPORT_RESPONSE_TIME}}. Reports of objectionable content sent through the app are reviewed faster; we aim to review them within 24 hours.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 (or 112). Fancy support is not an emergency service.

Report a member, a meal or a message

Open the member's profile or the meal and choose “Report this profile” or “Report this meal”. To report something said in a chat, press and hold the message and choose “Report message”. Tell us what happened in a few words. Your report is private and you receive a reference so you can check its status. You can also block a member from their profile to stop private contact.

Signing in

  • Email code: enter your email address and we send a six-digit code from mail.fancyabite.uk. The app shows how long it stays valid. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam or junk folder, then request a new code.
  • Google or Apple: use the same method you used before, so you return to the same account.

Your account and information

  • Edit your profile: Profile → Edit profile, or Settings → Edit public profile. You can add, change or remove your photo at any time.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. You can check progress in Settings → Check deletion status.
  • Analytics: turn optional analytics on or off in Settings.
  • Your data rights: to ask for a copy of your information, or to correct, restrict or object to how it is used, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account. See the Privacy Notice.

Meals and money

Fancy does not take payments. Hosts book the restaurant themselves and everyone pays the restaurant directly. If a booking or a meal changes, contact your host in the group chat. Talk to the restaurant directly about allergies and dietary needs.
