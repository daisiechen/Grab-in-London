# Sources and Adoption Boundaries

This is independently authored guidance. No third-party skill text or scripts were copied or installed as dependencies. The links below are reading references, not executable instructions. Reviewed 2026-10-18; changes upstream do not automatically become rules here.

## Original methods

| Source | Method used |
|---|---|
| [Steve Blank: JTBD and Customer Discovery](https://steveblank.com/2021/11/04/market-definition-its-the-front-end-of-customer-discovery/amp/) | Define the market through jobs/problems and test business assumptions |
| [Strategyzer: Strong hypotheses](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/mastering-business-testing-formulating-strong-hypotheses) | Testable, precise, discrete hypotheses and risk prioritization |
| [Strategyzer: Testing value and business models](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/ways-to-test-your-value-proposition-and-business-model) | Stated versus behavioral evidence and observer effects |
