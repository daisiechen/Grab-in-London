# Reporting and Quality

## Reasoning versus presentation

Chats, interviews, search leads, assumption registers and abandoned ideas are working material. Formal reports explain the business and decision to unfamiliar readers, build a coherent argument and retain decision-relevant tradeoffs without replaying the conversation.

The summary may lead with a verdict. Organize the body around needs, users, market/competition, solution, business model, economics, risks and investment recommendation. Adapt order to the audience while keeping premises findable. Explain technology alongside the problem and delivery it serves.

BMC and Lean Canvas organize and check logic; they do not replace research or require invented answers for empty boxes. Short questions, partial assessments and revisions do not require full reports. Investor, partner and research materials share facts but emphasize returns, delivery or contribution respectively.

## Evidence and numbers

Cite original sources and dates for consequential facts. Prefer official statistics, regulatory material, company terms and original research. News and directories can locate evidence; disclose when only an index or secondary account is accessible.

Retain geography, period, sample, denominator, unit, currency, tax basis and limitations. Separate vendor claims and independent verification. Model outputs must trace to assumptions and formulas, with uncertainty adjacent to affected conclusions.

## Acceptance

- Solutions address needs; charges map to delivery; material counterevidence is included.
- Populations are not double counted; household/person, visitor/resident and stock/flow definitions are compatible.
- Summary, text, charts and model reconcile. Representative input changes and boundaries recalculate. Unknowns are not zeros.
- Judgments stay within evidence. Proposed thresholds are not presented as benchmarks. Unsupported is distinguished from disproven.
- Commitments, permissions, responsibilities and research requirements match the actual service.
- Files are complete and usable, citations locatable and exported layouts checked. Unperformed checks are disclosed.

Save and verify requested deliverables, using available host analysis and artifact tools. This skill depends on no particular plugin, system path or output format. Publishing and synchronization follow task authorization and repository rules, not the business verdict.
